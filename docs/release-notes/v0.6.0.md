# Aura Farm v0.6.0

The app is now **Aura Farm**: a new session, a smarter form check, and a new look. The rubber chicken is still the coach.

## What's new

**A new session**
- The screen turns landscape by itself. The camera shows as a viewfinder with green corner marks on its edges, a faint blue ghost skeleton showing where to be,
  and camera-style controls on the right: a red record button to start, flip camera, close.
- Hold a plank for about a second and the camera zooms away into a field with a barn. You are a gold skeleton on the grass in the middle of the
  screen, and the scene follows how you turn the phone. Only a red timer and a stop button stay on screen.
- When you hit the minute, a golden egg pops, a banner slides in, and the phone turns portrait to show your card, which now shows the place you planked.

**A smarter form check**
- Shoulder, hip and knee are now checked as one line, shown as three dots on the skeleton. **All fours and knees on the floor no longer count** as a plank; a knee plank with your hips in line still does.

**Reminders and backups**
- Daily reminders at 6:00 and 13:00, and a 21:00 streak saver for when you have a streak to lose. They are off until you turn them on in the menu (Daily reminder).
- **Back up data** and **Restore data** in the menu save and restore every plank and replay as one file.

**A new look**: a game-style home screen, streak, banner and done screen, and a new icon (a slanted AF, with an aura glow, over a barn).

## Upgrading from Coach Chicken: please read

Aura Farm is a **different app to Android** (it has a new package name, `com.aurafarm`), so it installs **next to** Coach Chicken instead of over it. To keep your planks and streak:

1. In **Coach Chicken**, open the menu, tap **Back up data**, and save the file somewhere you can find it (for example Downloads).
2. Install **Aura Farm** (below).
3. In **Aura Farm**, open the menu, tap **Restore data**, and choose that file. It adds to what is there and never overwrites. Check your calendar.
4. Uninstall Coach Chicken, so you do not get reminders from both apps.

Do this in order: don't uninstall Coach Chicken until the restore has worked. If you have never installed Coach Chicken, ignore this.

## Install

1. On an Android phone (8.0 or newer, 64-bit ARM), download **aura-farm-v0.6.0.apk** below.
2. Open it and allow installing from your browser or file manager when asked.
3. Open **Aura Farm**, tap **Start session**, allow the camera, prop the phone side-on, and plank.

Pose detection runs on the phone, and the app's own code does not store or send the camera picture. The APK is about 30 MB because it bundles the pose-detection model.
One thing to know: the pose-detection library (Google's MediaPipe) includes a Google telemetry component, so the app lists the Internet permission. We have not yet audited what it sends, if anything, and removing the permission is planned.
It is signed with a stable key, so later releases will install over this one.

## Known issues

- Which way the scene moves when you turn the phone has not yet been confirmed on a device. If it moves the wrong way, please say so.
- The knee check (150°) is tuned on a small amount of data. If a good plank is marked wrong, please send a pose log (menu, **Copy pose log**, straight after the session).
- A portrait session from a distance is untested.
- The app holds the Internet permission through the pose-detection library, as above; we are looking at removing it.
- Small debug numbers (`y p r`) still show on the session screen.
